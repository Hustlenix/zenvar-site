Operating principles

Three questions, every decision.

Before we start, continue, or ship anything, we run it through these three. They keep an agency of seven people acting like a focused team of one.

P / 01
Signal over noise

Spend time on work that moves a client result or a team metric forward. Planning and looking busy are not signal. Every task earns its place or it drops.

P / 02
Attack the bottleneck

Before starting new work, identify what actually limits progress, then fix that first. Adding more to a stalled system only makes the jam worse.

P / 03
Numbers over feelings

“I think I improved” is not an update. Bring the number: leads, revenue, deliverables, deadlines hit. The number settles every argument.

In practice

How the questions get asked.

WEEKLY
One number each

Every person reports the one number they own. No status theatre.

BEFORE NEW WORK
Is this the bottleneck?

If it is not the tightest constraint, it waits.

AT REVIEW
Did the number move?

If not, we change the work and the owner stays accountable.
